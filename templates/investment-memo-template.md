# Investment Memo Template

Target length: **8–10 pages**. Written as a research note, not an essay.

Guidance on using this: replace every bracketed prompt with your own analysis and delete the
instructional italics. Lead with your conclusion — readers should know your recommendation from the
first sentence, not the last page.

# Writing Notes

*Delete this section before delivery.*

**Structure**
- Conclusion first. Every section should support the thesis stated on page one.
- One idea per paragraph. Lead each with its point.
- Charts must earn their place — if it doesn't support the thesis, cut it.

**Tone**
- Write with conviction but not certainty. "I expect" and "I estimate", not "will definitely".
- Quantify wherever you can. "Margins expand 200bps" beats "margins improve".
- Avoid hedging everything — a memo that never commits is useless.

**Common failures**
- Describing the company instead of arguing a thesis
- No differentiated view (nothing the market doesn't already know)
- Risk section as an afterthought
- False precision — a target price of ₹1,247.83 signals you don't understand your own error bars
- Assumptions you cannot defend when questioned

**The test:** can you deliver the thesis in 30 seconds, from memory, and have it land? If not, the
thesis isn't sharp enough yet.
