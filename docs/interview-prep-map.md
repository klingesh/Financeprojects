# Interview Prep Map

Which project answers which interview question. Use this to convert build work into interview
answers — and to identify gaps before someone else does.

The advantage of learning technicals by building is that your answers come with a concrete example
attached. "Yes, and when I built this for UltraTech, the thing that surprised me was..." is a far
stronger answer than a recited definition.

## The Stock Pitch

Asked in essentially every finance interview. Your capstone memo is the preparation.

Structure your answer:

1. **Recommendation and target** — one sentence, lead with the conclusion.
2. **Three reasons** — the thesis, in order of importance.
3. **Why the market is wrong** — what do you see that consensus doesn't? Without this, you have a
   description rather than a thesis.
4. **Catalyst** — what makes this play out, and when.
5. **Risks and what disproves you** — name the two things that would change your mind.

Practise at three lengths: **30 seconds**, **90 seconds**, and **5 minutes**. Interviewers cut you
off at different points and you need to survive all of them.

Bring a printed copy of the memo. Almost nobody does, and it changes the tenor of the conversation.

## Behavioural — "Why this project?"

You will be asked why you built this. Have a real answer:

> "I wanted to learn modelling by doing it rather than reading about it, so I built a full valuation
> of UltraTech from the annual reports — three-statement model, DCF, comps, then LBO and merger
> models. Then I did Citigroup separately, because banks need a completely different approach and I
> wanted to understand why. The thing that surprised me most was [specific, genuine observation]."

Fill that bracket with something you actually discovered. Interviewers can tell instantly whether
you built the model or downloaded it, and the tell is always whether you have a real observation
about the business.

## Known gaps in this portfolio

Be honest about these rather than being caught out:

- **No restructuring or distressed work** — no waterfall recovery analysis or fulcrum security work.
- **No industry-specific models beyond cement and banking** — real estate NAV, oil & gas reserve
  models and insurance embedded value are all untouched.
- **Limited market context** — you've built models, not tracked live markets. Read a financial daily
  and be able to discuss current rate and deal environments.
- **India-weighted** — four of six deliverables are Indian; only Citi is US. Adjust emphasis
  depending on which market you're recruiting in.
